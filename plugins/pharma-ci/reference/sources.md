# Sources for a pharma intelligence watchlist

Organise sources around the decision and reviewer. A source-page change is evidence to inspect;
its business or clinical significance requires a separate assessment. These categories come from
the workshop's practitioner research and selected monitoring configurations. They do not establish
adoption rates or how much of a team's intelligence comes from each source.

## The five jobs

| Job | The change to review | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Trial and pipeline | Status, dates, eligibility, arms, endpoints, results disclosures and phase changes | Known study records, scoped registry searches, competitor pipeline pages |
| Regulatory | Approvals, draft-to-final guidance, advisory calendars, warning letters and product records | FDA, EMA and national-agency pages |
| Congress | New sessions and abstracts, late-breaker lists and session-time changes | Current-cycle program and abstract portals |
| Market access and HTA | Product/country decisions, appraisal status, guideline versions and coverage criteria | NICE, G-BA/IQWiG, CDA-AMC, HAS, ICER, NCCN and payer pages |
| Launch and commercial | Brand/HCP claims, wording and imagery; provider pricing, availability and prominence | Brand/HCP sites, provider pages, newsrooms and public promotional materials |

## Match the page to the question

| Input | What it supports | Coverage check |
|---|---|---|
| Known record | Amendments to a study, product or guideline | Confirm identifier, captured fields and page sections |
| List or scoped search | Discovery of newly posted studies, approvals, sessions or documents | Confirm query, filters, sort order, pagination and loaded results |
| Linked document | Assessment of its content | Open or separately monitor the document; a list-page prompt sees captured list text |
| Brand/HCP or provider page | Published claims and commercial presentation | Choose the captured region and whether small text/image edits must reach review |

After discovering a new record or document, decide whether later amendments need a separate
monitor. An importance rule alone does not recursively read a site or add new URLs.

## Other sources to consider when they serve the decision

| Source | Review question | Corroboration |
|---|---|---|
| Careers pages | Does a relevant role or location change launch-preparation assumptions? | Job description, stated remit and company plans |
| Leadership pages | Does a change affect the team responsible for the watched program? | Appointment announcement and responsibilities |
| Investor materials | Has disclosed program timing or strategy changed? | Presentation/transcript itself and official records |
| SEC filings | Does the document change a disclosed risk, status or expectation? | Read the filing; a new link alone establishes availability |
| Payer bulletins/formularies | Did coverage criteria or tier change for the drug? | Exact policy, effective date and eligible population |
| Orange Book/patent registers | Did a listed patent or exclusivity entry change? | Product record and official entry |
| TV-ad archives/app listings | Has promotional material or a listing changed? | Actual ad, release note or listing |

These sources are optional. A vacancy alone does not establish a launch or the reason for hiring.
No source is required merely to complete a checklist.

## Public URL patterns

Patterns with placeholders are incomplete inputs. Verify the intended page before setup; never
guess a study, application or company identifier.

- Study record: `https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT________`
- Registry search: `https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?spons=...` or `?cond=...&intr=...`
- EU CTIS: `https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2024-______-__-__`
- FDA annual list: `https://www.fda.gov/drugs/novel-drug-approvals-fda/novel-drug-approvals-2026`
- Drugs@FDA: `https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=______`
- Orange Book: `https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/ob/`
- Advisory calendar: `https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar`
- EMA product: `https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/<name>`
- CHMP: `https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/committees/chmp`
- NICE: `https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/published`
- SEC list: `https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=<identifier>&type=8-K`
- Congress portals: verify the current-cycle program and abstract pages for the meeting in scope.
- Brand/provider pages: verify the exact product, treatment, efficacy or pricing page for the project.

## Maintain coverage

Record the reviewer, backup and maintenance owner. The maintenance owner checks failed captures,
expired access, moved URLs, annual page changes and newly published pages. Agree how discovery
works, when the source list is rechecked and who approves additions. New-page discovery needs its
own process or verified configuration.

## Separate source facts from interpretation

A changed endpoint is an observed field edit. Its reason requires checking record history,
protocol and other evidence. A new dose arm raises a study-design question; it establishes no
clinical outcome by itself. An announcement establishes what was published. The reviewer checks
product scope, location, date and relevance before proposing action.
